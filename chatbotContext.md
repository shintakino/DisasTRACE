## **1. General Disaster Preparedness**

#### **Category:** General Disaster Preparedness

**Intent:** disaster_preparedness **Possible Questions:**

- Ano ang disaster preparedness?

- What is disaster preparedness?

- Paano maghanda sa disaster?

- Ano ang dapat gawin bago ang disaster?

#### **Answer:**

Disaster preparedness means preparing yourself, your family, and your community before a disaster happens. This includes preparing emergency supplies, knowing evacuation routes, keeping important contact numbers, monitoring official announcements, and creating an emergency plan.

#### **Category:** General Disaster Preparedness

**Intent:** emergency_plan

#### **Possible Questions:**

- Paano gumawa ng emergency plan?

- Ano ang dapat nasa emergency plan?

- How do I make an emergency plan?

- Kailangan ba namin ng family emergency plan?

#### **Answer:**

An emergency plan should include important emergency contacts, evacuation routes, meeting places, communication methods, emergency supplies, and the specific needs of family members. Everyone in the household should know the plan and practice it when possible.

#### **Category:** General Disaster Preparedness

**Intent:** disaster_before

#### **Possible Questions:**

- Ano ang dapat gawin bago ang disaster?

- Paano maghanda bago ang sakuna?

- What should I do before a disaster?

- Ano ang dapat ihanda bago ang bagyo?

#### **Answer:**

Before a disaster, monitor official announcements, prepare your emergency supplies and go-bag, secure your home and surroundings, identify safe areas and evacuation routes, and make sure your family knows what to do.

#### **Category:** Emergency Supplies

**Intent:** go_bag

**Possible Questions:**

- Ano ang go-bag?

equipment, outlets, wires, or appliances that are wet or submerged. Move away from the area and seek assistance from appropriate authorities.

- Ano gagawin kapag lumindol?

### **19.0 PRIORITY OF THE CHATBOT**

When a user is actively reporting an incident, the chatbot should prioritize:

1. Immediate safety

2. Incident classification

3. Missing incident information

4. Completion of the report

5. General DisasTRACE / disaster information

6. Unrelated questions

The chatbot should be helpful with general questions, but it should always return the conversation to the active incident report when one is in progress.

#### GUIDE

DISASTRACE CHATBOT

#### 1. What can the chatbot do?

The DISASTRACE Chatbot can:

- Answer frequently asked questions.

- Provide general information about DISASTRACE.

- Explain how to use the application's features.

- Guide users on how to report an incident.

- Provide information about ambulance requests.

- Provide information about emergency contacts.

- Answer general emergency and safety questions.

- Guide users to the appropriate feature within the application.

#### 2. What are the limitations of the chatbot?

The chatbot is designed to provide general information and guidance. It does not replace:

- Emergency responders

- Medical professionals

- Police officers

- Firefighters

- Other authorized emergency personnel

For serious or immediate emergencies, users should use the appropriate emergency reporting or ambulance request feature or contact the proper emergency authorities.

#### 3. Can the chatbot respond to questions outside DISASTRACE?

The chatbot may provide answers to questions related to its available knowledge base, particularly:

#### * DISASTRACE

- Emergency preparedness

- Basic safety guidance

- Incident reporting

- Ambulance response

- Emergency contacts

For questions outside its scope, the chatbot should inform the user that it has limited information on that topic.

#### Example response:

I'm sorry, but I can currently assist you with questions related to DISASTRACE, emergency reporting, ambulance requests, safety information, and emergency contacts.

4. What happens if the chatbot does not understand the question?

If the chatbot cannot understand the user's question, it should ask the user to rephrase or provide suggested topics.

Example response:

I'm sorry, I didn't fully understand your question. Could you please rephrase it? You can ask me about DISASTRACE, reporting an incident, requesting an ambulance, emergency contacts, or safety information.

Yes. You can access the chatbot while filling out an incident report to ask for information or assistance. Your progress in the report should remain available when you return to the reporting form.
